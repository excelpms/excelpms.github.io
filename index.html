Start with the structure of the whole project.

Build the WBS, task weights and predecessor relationships in Project Control for design, procurement and manufacturing. VDR Planner develops issue, review and resubmission milestones and publishes document plans one way to VDR and PC.

Tasks advance. Documents move between parties.

Maintain actual dates and progress for ordinary procurement and manufacturing tasks. Use VDR for engineering issues, client reviews and sub-vendor documents, and GDR for clarifications, meeting records and other correspondence, with filing and transmittals.

03 / Measure

Turn execution into project progress.

Engineering documents earn progress under type, issue and review Code rules; ordinary tasks have maintained actual progress. Both roll up by WBS weights. Record current actuals at the report date and reconstruct history when corrections or late records require it.

Questions a project manager asks

What should you be able to find in PMS?

01

Can the schedule connect the work?

WBS, dependencies, calendars and Gantt charts organize design, procurement and manufacturing tasks.

02

How do revised plans compare?

Maintain Baseline and Re-schedule separately and compare independent actual data against the selected schedule basis.

Where is progress falling behind?
